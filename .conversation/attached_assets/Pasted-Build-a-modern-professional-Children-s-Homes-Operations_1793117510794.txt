Build a modern, professional Children’s Homes Operations Management Platform for an organization managing 4–10 children’s homes in Nairobi, Kenya.

The goal is to give the CEO one clear view of how every home is performing while enabling home managers to manage daily operations without constant manual follow-ups.

Design direction: Create a beautiful, polished, premium-looking application with a calm, welcoming aesthetic. Use a clean dashboard, thoughtful typography, a refined color palette, intuitive navigation, attractive data visualizations, clear status indicators and responsive layouts that work beautifully on both mobile and desktop. Make it feel like a real, production-quality SaaS product, not a generic admin template. Use your design creativity.

Core features:

- CEO dashboard: Overview of all homes, expenses, budgets, stock levels, outstanding issues, upcoming bills and weekly reporting compliance.
- Home management: Individual home profiles, managers and operational summaries.
- Finance: Record expenses, manage budgets, track payments and require CEO approval for expenses above a configurable organization-wide threshold, with optional home-specific overrides.
- Inventory: Track food, toiletries, cleaning products and other supplies, including quantities, costs, consumption and transfers between homes. Highlight low stock.
- Recurring charges: Track upcoming bills, due dates, payment status and reminders.
- Issues and communication: Managers can raise issues, attach relevant files, exchange private comments with the CEO or assigned people, track status and deadlines, and receive updates. Support priorities and urgent escalation.
- Weekly reports: Summarize finances, inventory, issues and reporting compliance for each home and the organization.
- Notifications: In-app and email notifications for important events and reminders.

User flow: The CEO signs in and sees the organization's overall position, with the ability to drill into any home, approve expenses, resolve outstanding issues and review reports. A home manager signs in and sees only their home's operations, records and assigned issues. They record expenses, update stock, track bills, submit weekly reports and raise issues when they need assistance.

Implementation: Build a genuinely functional full-stack application with persistent database storage, authentication, backend-enforced permissions, working forms and real calculations. Use a sensible, maintainable technology stack and prioritize affordable deployment. Start with realistic demo data, clearly separated from production data.

Build an impressive first version, then iterate. Make thoughtful product decisions, use your creativity, and prioritize a smooth user experience alongside reliable functionality. Test the main workflows instead of leaving them as visual mockups.
